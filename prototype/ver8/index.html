About this portrait & prototype

The galaxy samples the actual mapped question visuals. Domain directions and camera motion use shared rules. Q23 selections enlarge topic footprints; Q24 invitations move topics outward. These do not change sharing permissions. Missing answers and pending mappings add no invented shapes. Selves are perspectives across situations; interests and later narrative studies without a self assignment appear only under All selves. Empty profiles are hidden in filtered sky views.

Click a galaxy to open its profile. Shift-click two galaxies (or use Select two galaxies) to reveal their eligible connections in the sky. Hover or focus a line for a short explanation; click it for the original evidence. Lines distinguish exact numeric responses within the same question and self, shared categorical choices, and explicitly assigned shared reasons. A line does not measure similarity or relationship strength. No approximate matches or keyword guesses are used. Name-side dots and numbers count exact evidence items in the active filters, not unique topics or relationship strength. Click empty sky to clear selection.

All fourteen records are available for local researcher inspection. In this local test, all profiles may enter pair comparison even if their recorded Q37 scope is blank or restrictive. The original survey scope remains visible in each topic and is not changed. Story-card permissions remain separate. This test override is not participant consent for sharing or publication.

Unmapped answers remain inspectable in a single profile with their source. No data is sent to a server. Use the source guide in this folder to edit the individual rules.
